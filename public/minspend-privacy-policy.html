Privacy Policy — Minspend: Expense & Budget Tracker

Effective date: July 23, 2026

Minspend ("the app") is an expense and budget tracking app for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. The short version:

Your data stays on your device, and we do not collect, sell, or share any of it.

Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting SDKs. We (the developer) operate no servers and receive no data from your device.

Data stored on your device

Expenses you record — amount, date, category, type, notes, payment app, and optional merchant/transaction details — are stored in a local database on your device only. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

The app participates in Android's standard backup system, so your expense data may be included in your device's encrypted Google backup, under your own Google account and Google's backup terms.

Accessibility service (optional)

The app offers a floating "quick add" bubble that appears when you open a payment app you have chosen to enable. To do this, the app uses an Android accessibility service that:

  • Detects when one of your enabled payment apps comes to the foreground, so the bubble can be shown or hidden. Only the app's package name is checked for this.
  • Only if you separately turn on the opt-in "auto-read amount" setting, reads the payment amount shown on screen in enabled payment apps only, to pre-fill the expense entry form. The amount is held in memory, used once to pre-fill the form, and is never stored or transmitted anywhere unless you save the expense yourself.

The accessibility service does not read your screen in any other app, does not log screen content, and sends nothing off your device. It is entirely optional — the app works without it.

Google Sheets sync (optional)

You can optionally sync your expenses to your own Google Sheet by pasting a Google Apps Script URL that you create in your own Google account. When enabled, your expense records are sent directly from your device to that script URL over HTTPS. This connection is between your device and your own Google account — we are not involved and cannot access the spreadsheet or the data. Google's handling of that data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. If you never set up sync, the app makes no network requests with your data.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet — used only for the optional Google Sheets sync described above.
  • Display over other apps — shows the floating quick-add bubble.
  • Notifications / foreground service — keeps the bubble service running reliably and shows its status.

Data sharing and selling

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any data with third parties. There are no third-party analytics or advertising partners.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Data deletion

All data lives on your device. To delete it, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. If you used Google Sheets sync, you can delete the synced rows from your own spreadsheet at any time.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated and the effective date revised before the change ships.
