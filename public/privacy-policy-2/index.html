Privacy Policy — Baby Photo Editor

Effective date: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Baby Photo Editor ("the app") handles information when you use its photo-editing features.

Your photos are processed on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share your photos or personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app does not require an account, and we do not operate servers that receive your photos, edited images, or other personal data.

Photos and media

The app lets you select photos from your device so you can edit them with frames, filters, text, stickers, and other creative tools. Photos you select are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to us.

Original and edited photos remain under your control. The app accesses only the photos you choose or the media access you permit through your device settings.

Camera access

If you choose to take a new photo from within the app, the app may request access to your device camera. Camera access is used only to capture the photo you request. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.

Storage and permissions

Depending on your device and operating-system version, the app may request permission to:

  • Access photos or media — to open a photo you choose for editing.
  • Use the camera — to take a new photo when you select that option.
  • Save media — to store an edited image on your device.

These permissions are used only to provide the features you request. You can manage them at any time in your device settings, although disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

Saving and sharing

When you save an edited photo, it is stored on your device. If you choose to share it, the app uses your device's standard share menu. The service or app you select to receive the photo handles it according to its own privacy policy. We do not receive a copy.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your photos or personal information with third parties.

Data retention and deletion

We do not retain your photos or personal data on our servers. You can delete saved or edited photos using your device's photo gallery or file manager. You can remove the app and its locally stored settings by uninstalling it or clearing its storage in your device settings.

Children's privacy

The app is a photo-editing tool for parents, guardians, and other users. It is not intended for children under 13 to use independently, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Because photo editing takes place locally, your photos remain protected by your device's security controls. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we recommend keeping your device and operating system up to date.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or data practices change. Any update will appear on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email hello@vishwanathkarka.com.
